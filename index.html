Ongoing care — from $50 CAD/month

Your website, looked after every month

A website is not a one-time purchase. Prices change, menus change, photos go stale — and small things break at the worst possible moment. A care plan covers all of it for one flat monthly fee: hosting, backups, security monitoring and small content updates, plus repairs whenever something goes wrong. You email me, it gets done.

Plans run month to month with no contract. If you ever leave, the files, the domain and the hosting access are yours — no hostage situations.

Already have a website? Start with a rescue audit

Care plans work on sites I didn’t build too — WordPress, Wix, or something hand-coded years ago. The first step is a one-time Website Rescue Audit, $199 CAD flat: I check security, speed, mobile layout, forms and your Google setup, fix whatever is critical, and put backups and monitoring in place. You get a written report of what I found.

Emergency repairs are covered by a plan only after that audit — so a five-year-old plugin can’t turn into a surprise ten-hour rebuild on a $50 month. And if the audit shows the site is beyond saving, I credit the $199 toward a new build.

Keeping your own hosting? The plan price stays the same — managing your hosting panel is part of the work.

Questions owners ask before signing up

What counts as a small content change?

Updating text, prices, hours or staff names, swapping photos, adding or editing a menu item, adding a review, fixing a typo or a broken link, and small banner refreshes. One request counts as one change.

What if I need something bigger?

You get a flat price first, and nothing happens without your OK. An extra change outside your plan is $75 CAD flat, a new page is $250 CAD flat, and Complete already includes one new page or section every quarter.

Can I cancel my care plan?

Yes. Plans are month to month with no contract and no cancellation fee. If you leave, the files, the domain and the hosting access are transferred to you.

My site was built by someone else. Can I still sign up?

Yes. It moves onto a care plan after a one-time Website Rescue Audit of $199 CAD flat, which fixes what is critical and sets up backups and monitoring. Emergency repairs are covered only after that audit, and the fee is credited toward a new build if the site is beyond saving.

What happens if my site breaks at 2 a.m.?

Uptime and security are monitored around the clock and emergency repairs are included in every plan, so putting the site back online never generates an extra invoice. You can reach me any day of the week; replies land within 2 business days on Essential, 1 on Plus and the same day on Complete.
